# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-003
**Version:** 2.4
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All regular employees.

---

## 1. Work location categories

Every employee record in WorkWeek carries exactly one `work_location` value. It is the authoritative source for every entitlement in this policy and in the Expense and Equipment Policy (HR-POL-002).

| `work_location` value | Meaning | Office attendance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `On-site` | Works from a company office | 5 days per week |
| `Hybrid-<city>` | Split between office and home | Minimum 2 days per week in the named office |
| `Remote-<country>` | Works from home in the named country | Optional, no minimum |

Examples of valid values: `On-site`, `Hybrid-London`, `Hybrid-Singapore`, `Remote-UK`, `Remote-DE`.

**Self-declaration does not change entitlement.** An employee who states they work remotely but whose record says `On-site` is treated as on-site until the record is changed by People Operations.

---

## 2. Eligibility for remote and hybrid status

Remote or hybrid status requires all of the following:

1. Six months of continuous service, waived for roles hired explicitly as remote.
2. A role that does not require physical presence, as determined by the department head.
3. Manager approval recorded in WorkWeek.
4. A work location in a country where the company has a legal employing entity.

Changes to `work_location` are made by People Operations. The assistant cannot change it, and it cannot approve remote status.

---

## 3. Equipment entitlement

Employees whose `work_location` begins with **Remote** or **Hybrid** are entitled to home office equipment as set out in HR-POL-002, Section 3.2. The headline entitlement is:

* **One external monitor of up to 27 inch, once every 3 years.**
* One keyboard and mouse set, once every 3 years.
* Desk chair up to USD 400.00 and desk up to USD 350.00, once every 5 years.

Requests are raised in ServiceImmediately under category **Hardware**, priority **4 - Low**, shipped to the personal address held in WorkWeek.

Employees whose `work_location` is `On-site` are **not** entitled to home office equipment.

---

## 4. Working hours and availability

Remote and hybrid employees keep the core hours of their assigned office: **10:00 to 16:00 local office time**. Outside core hours, working time is flexible provided commitments are met.

Employees notify their manager if they will be unavailable during core hours for more than 60 minutes.

---

## 5. Working from another location temporarily

### 5.1 Within the country of employment
Permitted without approval for any duration.

### 5.2 Outside the country of employment
Permitted for a maximum of **20 working days per calendar year**, and no more than **10 consecutive working days** in a single trip. It requires:

* Manager approval, and
* A ticket in ServiceImmediately under category **HR Access** stating the country and dates, raised at least **10 working days** in advance.

Longer periods create tax and employment-law exposure and are treated as a relocation under HR-POL-005.

### 5.3 Working during approved leave
Not permitted. An employee on approved leave is not expected to work, and working during leave does not restore leave days.

---

## 6. Security requirements

Remote and hybrid employees must:

1. Use the company-issued laptop for all company work. Personal devices are not permitted for accessing company systems.
2. Use the company VPN when connecting from a network that is not their registered home network.
3. Keep the two-factor security key physically separate from the laptop when travelling.
4. Not work on company systems from a shared or public computer under any circumstances.
5. Report a lost or stolen device to ServiceImmediately within **4 hours** of discovery, priority **1 - Critical**.

---

## 7. Office space

Remote employees do not have an assigned desk. Hybrid employees book a desk through the facilities system. Visiting a different office requires a building access request in ServiceImmediately under category **Facilities**, raised at least **3 working days** in advance.
