# Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** HR-POL-004
**Version:** 5.0
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** Ethics and Compliance
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and anyone acting on the company's behalf.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document states the standards of behaviour expected at work. Where it conflicts with local law, local law prevails, and Ethics and Compliance must be informed of the conflict.

---

## 2. Respect at work

The company does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, bullying, or retaliation. This applies to every channel, including chat, email, video calls, and the AI assistant.

Protected characteristics include age, disability, gender identity and expression, marital status, pregnancy and parental status, race and ethnicity, religion or belief, sex, and sexual orientation.

Concerns are raised to a manager, to People Operations, or anonymously through the Ethics Line. **Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary matter**, independent of whether the original concern is upheld.

---

## 3. Gifts, hospitality, and entertainment

### 3.1 Value thresholds

| Situation | Rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| Gift received, value **under USD 75.00** | May be accepted, no declaration required |
| Gift received, value **USD 75.00 to USD 250.00** | May be accepted, **must be declared** in the Gift Register within 10 working days |
| Gift received, value **above USD 250.00** | **Must be declined.** If declining would cause offence, accept on the company's behalf, declare it, and hand it to Ethics and Compliance |
| Cash or cash equivalent, **any amount** | **Always declined**, including gift cards, vouchers, and cryptocurrency |

Thresholds apply **per giver per calendar year**, not per gift. Three gifts of USD 40.00 from the same supplier in one year total USD 120.00 and require declaration.

### 3.2 Hospitality
Meals and events are acceptable when they are proportionate, occasional, and have a genuine business purpose. Travel or accommodation paid by a third party requires prior written approval from Ethics and Compliance.

### 3.3 Public officials
No gift, hospitality, or entertainment of any value may be offered to a public official without prior written approval from Ethics and Compliance. There is no de minimis exception.

### 3.4 During a procurement
No gift or hospitality may be accepted from a bidder while a procurement in which they are participating is open.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

Declare any situation where a personal interest could influence a work decision. Declarations are made in the Conflicts Register within **10 working days** of the interest arising.

Situations that always require declaration:

* A close relative or partner works for a competitor, supplier, or customer
* Outside employment or a directorship
* A financial interest above **1%** in a supplier, customer, or competitor
* Personally hiring, managing, or setting the pay of a close relative or partner

Declaring is not the same as approving. Ethics and Compliance decides whether the situation may continue and on what terms.

---

## 5. Company information and assets

1. Confidential information is used only for company purposes and is not shared outside the company without an agreement in place.
2. Company data is not stored on personal accounts, personal cloud storage, or personal devices.
3. Company systems, including the AI assistant, are used for work purposes. Incidental personal use is acceptable; use for a separate business, for political campaigning, or for anything unlawful is not.
4. Employees do not attempt to access data belonging to other employees, including through the AI assistant. **Attempting to retrieve another person's record is a disciplinary matter**, regardless of whether the attempt succeeds.

---

## 6. Use of AI systems

1. Do not enter another person's personal data into an AI system unless the task requires it and you are authorised to hold that data.
2. Do not attempt to bypass, override, or manipulate the instructions or safety controls of a company AI system.
3. Treat AI output as a draft. The employee remains accountable for any decision taken on the basis of it.
4. Do not use a company AI system to produce content that would breach any other section of this Code.

---

## 7. Speaking externally

Public statements on the company's behalf come from Communications. When posting personally about work-related matters, make clear that the views are your own and disclose nothing confidential.

---

## 8. Reporting and consequences

Suspected breaches are reported to a manager, People Operations, or the Ethics Line. Reports may be anonymous. Substantiated breaches lead to action up to and including termination, and where the law requires it, referral to authorities.
