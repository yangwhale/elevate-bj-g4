# Leave Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-001
**Version:** 4.2
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All regular full-time and part-time employees. Contractors are covered by their engagement terms, not by this policy.

---

## 1. Leave types recorded in WorkWeek

WorkWeek records exactly two leave categories. All other absence types described in this policy are recorded under one of these two.

| Category in WorkWeek | Covers |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Vacation** | Annual leave, personal days, floating holidays |
| **Sick** | Illness, medical appointments, short-term medical leave, bereavement |

Employees never select a leave category not listed above. A request for bereavement or short-term medical leave is submitted as **Sick**.

---

## 2. Vacation leave

### 2.1 Accrual
Regular full-time employees accrue **1.67 days per completed calendar month**, giving **20 working days per full calendar year**. Part-time employees accrue pro rata to contracted hours.

Accrual begins on the first day of employment. Leave may be taken before it has accrued only with written manager approval, and the balance may not go below **-5.0 days** at any time.

### 2.2 Carry-over
A maximum of **5.0 unused vacation days** may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried days expire on **31 March**. Days above the cap are forfeited on 31 December and are not paid out.

### 2.3 Notice
| Length of request | Minimum notice |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1–2 working days | 2 working days |
| 3–9 working days | 10 working days |
| 10 or more working days | 30 calendar days |

Notice may be waived by the employee's manager. The assistant does not waive notice; it submits the request and the manager approves or declines in WorkWeek.

### 2.4 Limits
A single request may not exceed **20 working days**. Requests exceeding the employee's remaining accrued balance are rejected at submission. Start dates in the past are rejected. A start date after the end date is rejected.

---

## 3. Sick leave

### 3.1 Entitlement
Regular employees receive **10 paid sick days per calendar year**, granted in full on 1 January and not accrued monthly. Unused sick days **do not carry over** and are not paid out.

### 3.2 Certification
A medical certificate is required for any absence of **4 or more consecutive working days**. Certificates are submitted to People Operations directly. **Employees are never asked to provide a diagnosis**, and diagnosis information must not be recorded in any HR system or support ticket.

### 3.3 Exhausted sick balance
Once the 10-day entitlement is exhausted, further medical absence is handled under Section 5, Short-term medical leave.

---

## 4. Bereavement leave

Bereavement leave is paid and is **granted in addition to** the sick leave entitlement in Section 3. It does not reduce the sick balance.

| Relationship | Paid working days |
| :--- | :---: |
| Immediate family: spouse or domestic partner, child, parent, sibling | **5** |
| Extended family: grandparent, grandchild, parent-in-law, sibling-in-law | **3** |
| Other close relationship, at manager discretion | **1** |

Days must be taken within **30 calendar days** of the date of death unless the funeral or memorial occurs later, in which case they may be taken within 30 days of that event. Bereavement leave is recorded in WorkWeek as **Sick** with the note field set to `Bereavement`.

Travel of more than 500 km for the funeral entitles the employee to **1 additional paid day**, requested through the manager.

---

## 5. Short-term medical leave

### 5.1 Definition
A continuous medical absence of **more than 10 working days** and up to **12 weeks**.

### 5.2 Procedure
1. Notify the manager as early as practicable, and no later than the first day of absence.
2. Submit a medical certificate to People Operations covering the expected period.
3. Submit the absence in WorkWeek as **Sick** for the full expected duration.
4. Raise a ticket in ServiceImmediately under category **HR Access** so that email and calendar are routed to a delegate for the duration.

Steps 3 and 4 are the only steps the assistant performs. Steps 1 and 2 remain with the employee, because they involve a manager conversation and a medical document.

### 5.3 Pay
The first **10 working days** are paid at 100% from the sick entitlement in Section 3. Beyond that, statutory or insured disability benefit applies according to the employee's country of employment. This policy does not state benefit amounts; People Operations confirms them per case.

### 5.4 Return
Absences longer than **20 working days** require a fitness-to-return confirmation before the first day back.

---

## 6. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to **3 months** may be requested after **12 months** of service, subject to manager and People Operations approval. Unpaid leave is not recorded through the assistant; it requires a written request to People Operations.

---

## 7. Public holidays

Public holidays follow the employee's country and, where applicable, regional calendar. Public holidays falling inside an approved vacation period are **not** deducted from the vacation balance.

---

## 8. Cancellation

An approved but not yet started leave request may be cancelled by the employee at any time, and the days are returned to the balance immediately. A leave request that has already started may only be shortened by People Operations.
