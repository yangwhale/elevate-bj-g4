# Expense and Equipment Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-002
**Version:** 3.1
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** Finance Operations, with IT Asset Management for Section 4
**Applies to:** All employees and contractors submitting expenses or requesting company equipment.

---

## 1. Principles

1. An expense is reimbursable if it is necessary for the work, reasonable in amount, and not listed in Section 6.
2. Receipts are required for every claim of **USD 25.00 or more**. Below that threshold a description is sufficient.
3. Claims must be submitted within **60 calendar days** of the expense date. Claims older than that are declined without exception.
4. Pre-approval is required for any single item of **USD 500.00 or more**.

---

## 2. Standard issue equipment

Every employee receives the following at onboarding, at no cost and without a request:

| Item | Refresh cycle |
| :--- | :--- |
| Laptop | 3 years |
| Laptop charger and travel adapter | On failure |
| Headset for calls (wired or wireless, standard model) | 2 years |
| Two-factor security key | On failure |

Standard issue items are not expensed. They are requested through ServiceImmediately under category **Hardware**.

---

## 3. Home office equipment

### 3.1 Eligibility
Home office equipment is available to employees whose WorkWeek work location begins with **Remote** or **Hybrid**. Employees whose work location is **On-site** are not eligible; their equipment is provided at the office.

Eligibility is determined from the WorkWeek record, not from the employee's statement.

### 3.2 Entitlement

| Item | Entitlement | Refresh cycle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| External monitor, up to 27 inch | **1 per employee** | **3 years** |
| Keyboard and mouse | 1 set | 3 years |
| Desk chair, up to USD 400.00 | 1 | 5 years |
| Desk, up to USD 350.00 | 1 | 5 years |
| Monitor arm, cables, docking station | As needed | On failure |

A second monitor requires manager approval and a documented work requirement.

### 3.3 How to request
Home office equipment is requested through ServiceImmediately under category **Hardware**, priority **4 - Low**. The ticket must contain the shipping address, which is taken from the personal address on the WorkWeek record. Employees update that address in WorkWeek before raising the ticket if it is out of date.

Equipment remains company property and is returned on termination.

---

## 4. Peripherals and accessories

### 4.1 Noise-cancelling headphones
Noise-cancelling headphones are reimbursable up to **USD 250.00**, **once every 2 years**, for employees who meet **either** of the following:

* Work location begins with **Remote** or **Hybrid**, or
* Sit in an open-plan office area.

They are claimed as an expense with a receipt, not requested as hardware. A claim above USD 250.00 is reimbursed at USD 250.00 and the remainder is the employee's cost.

### 4.2 Other peripherals
Webcams, ring lights, USB hubs and cable accessories are reimbursable up to **USD 100.00 combined per calendar year** without pre-approval.

### 4.3 Mobile phones
The company does not reimburse personal mobile handsets. Employees in roles with an on-call rotation receive a company handset through IT Asset Management.

## 5. Travel and subsistence

| Category | Limit |
| :--- | :--- |
| Domestic hotel, per night | USD 200.00 |
| International hotel, per night | USD 280.00 |
| Meals, per day, domestic | USD 65.00 |
| Meals, per day, international | USD 85.00 |
| Economy flights | Required for all flights under 6 hours |
| Premium economy | Permitted for flights of 6 hours or more |
| Business class | Director level and above only, flights of 8 hours or more |
| Ground transport | Standard taxi or ride-share; car rental requires pre-approval |

Personal travel attached to a business trip is at the employee's cost, and the flight is reimbursed at the cost of the equivalent business-only itinerary.

---

## 6. Not reimbursable

The following are never reimbursable, regardless of amount or approval:

* Alcohol, except as part of an approved client entertainment event
* Traffic fines, parking fines and speeding penalties
* Personal grooming, clothing, and dry cleaning other than on trips of 7 or more nights
* Home broadband and home utilities
* Personal software subscriptions and personal cloud storage
* Gifts to family members
* Loss or theft of personal property
