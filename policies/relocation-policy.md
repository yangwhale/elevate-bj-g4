# Relocation Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-005
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People Operations, Global Mobility
**Applies to:** Regular employees relocating at the company's request or with company approval.

---

## 1. Scope

This policy covers a **permanent change of the employee's country or city of employment**. Temporary work from another location is covered by the Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-003), Section 5.

A relocation is company-initiated when the company requests the move, and employee-initiated when the employee requests it and the company agrees. Support differs between the two, as set out in Section 3.

---

## 2. Eligibility

| Condition | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Service | 12 months continuous service, waived for company-initiated moves |
| Approval | Receiving department head, releasing department head, and Global Mobility |
| Legal | Right to work in the destination country, confirmed before any commitment |
| Timing | Effective date at least 30 calendar days after final approval |

---

## 3. Relocation allowance

### 3.1 Allowance table

| Move type | Maximum allowance |
| :--- | :---: |
| Domestic, same country, over 80 km | **USD 8,000** |
| International, single employee | **USD 15,000** |
| International, employee with accompanying dependants | **USD 22,000** |

The allowance is a **maximum reimbursable amount, not a lump-sum payment**. It is claimed against receipts through the process in Section 5. Unused allowance is not paid out.

For **employee-initiated** relocations, the allowance is **50%** of the amounts above, and temporary accommodation in Section 3.2 is not provided.

### 3.2 What the allowance covers

| Covered | Not covered |
| :--- | :--- |
| Shipping of household goods, up to 20 cubic metres | Purchase of property |
| One-way economy flights for the employee and dependants | Loss on sale of property |
| Temporary accommodation, up to 30 nights | Furniture and appliance purchases |
| Visa, work permit and legal fees | Pet import quarantine fees above USD 1,000 |
| Destination-country tax advice, one session | Ongoing tax preparation |
| School search assistance | School fees |

### 3.3 Repayment
If the employee resigns within **12 months** of the effective date, the allowance is repayable on a sliding scale: 100% within 6 months, 50% between 6 and 12 months, nil thereafter. This does not apply where the company terminates the employment other than for cause.

---

## 4. Steps in a relocation

1. **Approval.** Recorded by Global Mobility. Nothing else begins until this is complete.
2. **Right to work.** Confirmed for the destination country.
3. **Update the WorkWeek record.** The employee updates the personal address and phone number. `work_location` is changed by People Operations, not by the employee and not by the assistant.
4. **Building access.** Raise a ticket in ServiceImmediately under category **Facilities**, priority **3 - Moderate**, at least **5 working days** before the first day at the new office.
5. **IT provisioning.** Raise a ticket under category **Hardware** if the destination requires a different power standard or keyboard layout.
6. **Payroll and tax.** Handled by Global Mobility. The employee takes no action.

Steps 3, 4 and 5 are the only steps the assistant performs. Steps 1, 2 and 6 involve approvals and legal checks that require a human.

---

## 5. Claiming

Claims are submitted through the standard expense process in HR-POL-002, tagged `RELOCATION`, with receipts for every item regardless of amount. The 60-day submission window in HR-POL-002 Section 1 runs from the **effective date of the relocation**, not from the date of each expense.

---

## 6. Leave associated with a relocation

Employees may take up to **3 paid days** for the move itself. These days are recorded in WorkWeek as **Vacation** with the note field set to `Relocation`, and are **not** deducted from the vacation balance. People Operations credits the days back after the move is confirmed.
